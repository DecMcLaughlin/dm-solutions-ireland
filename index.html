What we offer, and where each service stands

We only take on work when the people, supervision, insurance and contracts are in place. Each service below shows how far along it is.

Current focus

EHS professional supply

Suitably qualified and experienced Environmental, Health and Safety professionals for construction and engineering projects. Depending on competence and agreed scope: site safety monitoring, inspections, safety observations, RAMS review support, toolbox talks, incident reporting support and project safety documentation.

Developing

Skilled workforce solutions

Skilled and semi-skilled construction and engineering personnel: mechanical and electrical operatives, pipefitters, duct installers, cable installers and other project-specific trades, subject to verified competence, certification and client requirements.

Future

Mechanical services

Defined mechanical installation and subcontracting packages, undertaken only when competent personnel, technical supervision, insurance, systems and contractual arrangements are in place.

Future

Electrical services

Electrical service and subcontracting opportunities, scoped in line with applicable qualifications, authorisations, technical supervision, insurance and regulatory requirements.

Long term

Integrated project support

Workforce and specialist services coordinated through one accountable company relationship, so clients can respond to changing project demands.

How we work

Five steps from first conversation to the next job. The order matters, because each step depends on the one before it.

  1. UNDERSTAND

    Scope first

    Establish your scope, programme, priorities and requirements.

  2. ASSESS

    Match the people

    Identify the relevant people, competencies, resources and delivery arrangements.

  3. ALIGN

    Agree terms

    Agree responsibilities, expectations, commercial terms and project requirements.

  4. DELIVER

    Do the work

    Provide the agreed service professionally and within the agreed scope.

  5. DEVELOP

    Improve

    Seek feedback, strengthen the relationship and improve future delivery.

Two strangers, one conversation, one company

DM Solutions Ireland Limited began with a conversation between Ronan Devine and Felton Meth. It grew into a professional partnership and a shared aim: a dependable solutions partner that understands what a client needs and responds with competent people, practical services and clear communication.

The name is the partnership. D for Devine. M for Meth. It stands for shared ambition and mutual commitment, and we intend to earn it through trust, accountability and the value we deliver.

Sustainable growth comes from delivering value, maintaining trust and making commitments that match the capability available.
