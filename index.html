Our story

DM Solutions Ireland Limited was born from a conversation between Ronan Devine and Felton Meth, united by a shared vision to build a dependable personnel solutions partner.

Our aim is simple: to connect businesses with the right people — skilled, semi-skilled, trained and competent personnel who can support projects, strengthen teams and meet workforce demands.

D for Devine. M for Meth. One partnership, one vision, one commitment — supplying people clients can rely on.
